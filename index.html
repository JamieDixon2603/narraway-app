Feeling a Bit Lost?

Whether you're starting a new job, embarking on an unfamiliar project, or entering a new phase of life, change can leave you feeling uncertain.

Sometimes all you need is more clarity about where you're going, and more confidence that you already have what it takes to get there.

That's why NarraWay exists.

A Simple 3-Step Process

1

Identify Your Strengths

Reflect on what you're good at and choose equipment cards to represent those strengths. These are the tools you carry with you on every journey.

2

Map Your Journey

Build a visual map of your challenge or goal: your destination, your starting point, and the terrain you'll navigate in between.

3

Apply Your Strengths

Place your equipment where it will help most. See exactly how your existing strengths will carry you through each step of your journey.

Your Logbook

Your Logbook is the main output of the NarraWay experience. It captures everything you've discovered: your strengths, your map, your reflections, and your plan for the journey ahead.

At the end of each session, you can export your Logbook as a PDF. It's designed to be printed, kept, and returned to. A tangible record of your thinking at this moment in time, and a reference point as you move forward.
